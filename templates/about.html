Why we started this

Painting a tall building still means putting a person on a rope.

Exterior painting in India is done from suspended platforms, bamboo scaffolding and rope cradles. The work is slow, the finish varies from one worker to the next, and the fall risk is real and recurring. Every year, painters die doing a job that a machine could do instead.

Parameswar Reddy, 50

On 30 May 2026, a painter died after the suspended platform he was working from tilted on the third floor of a residential building under construction in Emmiganur, Kurnool district.

He is the reason this project exists.

Falls from height

Suspended platforms and rope access remain a leading cause of construction fatalities. Harnesses reduce the risk; they don't remove it.

Uneven finish

Hand spraying leaves patches too thick, too thin, or missed entirely, and quality changes with fatigue and with each worker.

The cost of access

On a tall wall, scaffolding and access can cost several times more than the painting labour itself, before any paint is opened.

How the machine works

An operator places the rover at the base of the wall, anchors a safety line, and works from a ground station. Paint and power run up a single umbilical line.

adhesion

Vacuum plenum

A fan continuously draws air out of a chamber behind a soft skirt, so outside air pressure presses the rover into the wall and friction holds it up. Because nothing depends on a sealed cup, it keeps gripping on rough, porous plaster where ordinary suction cups slip.

drive

Track drive

Tracks spread the load over a larger contact patch than wheels. That keeps the pressure differential stable while the rover is moving, not only while it is parked.

application

Airless spray arm

A nozzle on a short arm sprays while a distance sensor holds a fixed standoff from the wall, so film thickness stays even across the surface instead of drifting as the rover moves.

perception

Vision and coverage planning

An onboard camera picks out windows, pipes and edges to route around. The coverage planner divides the wall into cells and sweeps them back and forth, an approach that can be proven to reach every point rather than merely most of them.

safety

Interlocks

Grip pressure and tilt are checked continuously against fixed limits. A physical emergency stop and an independent safety line sit outside the software entirely, so a fault in the control system cannot take them away.

Where the project stands

The concept phase is complete. We are moving into mechanism selection and detailed design.

rev 0.1

Use case fixed

One target application chosen and scoped: flat vertical concrete and cement-plastered exterior walls.

rev 0.2

Requirements defined

Every performance and safety target written as a measurable number with a test method attached.

rev 0.3

Mechanism selection

Comparing adhesion, drive and spray options against the requirements, and sourcing components within the sanctioned budget.

rev 0.4

Design and prototype build

Mechanical CAD, electrical architecture and control design, then assembly of the first working rover.

rev 0.5

Testing on a wall

Adhesion and coverage trials on low walls first, moving higher only once reliability is demonstrated.
